A deliberately short, second source — different method from Zacks. Score = 45% analyst conviction + 45% earnings-beat consistency + 10% opinion drift. Sorted, not ranked as advice; no pick is asserted.

Ticker Company Sector Score i0–100 overall score = 45% Conviction + 45% Beats + 10% Drift, ×100. Example (AMT): 0,45×0,52 + 0,45×1,00 + 0,10×0,74 = 0,76 → 76. It's relative, not a probability — it orders your attention, it's not a verdict. Conviction iHow strongly analysts lean buy. (2×StrongBuy + Buy) ÷ (2×analysts) — strong-buys count double, holds/sells count 0. All strong-buy = 1,0; all plain buy = 0,5. Example (AMT): (2×7 + 16) ÷ (2×29) = 0,52. Beats Earnings-beat consistency. Share of the last 4 quarters where actual earnings beat the estimate. 4/4 = 1,0. A stock with no earnings coverage defaults to 0,5 (neutral). Drift iWhich way analyst opinion is moving, over the ~4 months Finnhub returns. ▲ improving → toward 1,0; flat = 0,5; ▼ cooling → toward 0.
